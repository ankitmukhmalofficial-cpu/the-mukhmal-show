# Future Enhancements Backlog

## Current status

The Mukhmall Show currently has an owner-managed Stories CMS, moderated customer feedback, YouTube synchronization, analytics, enquiry management, SEO metadata, social links, and a protected admin dashboard.

## Prioritized next steps

| Priority | Enhancement | Status | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Full Story CMS polish | Partly implemented | Owner story create, edit, publish, and draft controls already exist. A richer editor, cover images, tags, and scheduled publishing can be added next. |
| 2 | Consent and privacy center | Deferred | The site uses privacy-conscious visitor aggregation and phone hashing. A formal consent banner, privacy policy, and analytics preferences should be finalized together after the owner confirms the preferred policy text and retention period. |
| 3 | Admin export | Deferred | CSV export for enquiries, analytics, and feedback is useful, but it should include explicit owner authorization, masked fields by default, and an export audit trail. |
| 4 | Optional visitor login | Deferred | Login is not required for YouTube subscription or feedback submission. If added later, it should be optional, consent-based, and separate from YouTube subscription so the site never collects subscriber Gmail without a clear purpose. |

Customer reviews, ratings, and testimonials must remain genuine and owner-approved. No sample or fabricated customer content should be added while these enhancements are developed.
